$30

Our most expensive dish, but worth the price. A HEAVY plate with the perfect amount of sauce. Be warned: it is SPICY! I found myself taking breaks between bites. I'd recommend garlic bread for balance. Despite the spice, the flavor was excellent, with no bitterness from the vodka. My only complaints are that I'd prefer less heat and meat included, rather than an extra $7.

ATMOSPHERE

This place was BUSY! Almost every table was full around 8 PM, yet it wasn't loud at all. I loved the dim lighting, making it perfect for a date night. Just plan ahead, getting reservations are HARD!

SERVICE

Our service was great! I felt very well taken care of. Our server was kind and funny, even offering us a complimentary dessert and keeping us updated on our food's wait time.

ZEUS'S THOUGHTS

I loved the flavor of the sauce and kept wanting more, but the spice was definitely intense! Despite the heat, I really enjoyed it and would probably order it over the steak next time. However, if you're not a fan of spicy food, I'd recommend skipping this one.

★ ★ ★ ★ ★

LAST THOUGHTS

A heavy, flavorful plate that mostly earned its price. The sauce was perfect and the vodka had no bitterness, but the heat got intense enough that I needed breaks, and I wish meat had been included for the extra $7. With great service and a busy, dimly lit room, I'd happily come back, just book a reservation first.
