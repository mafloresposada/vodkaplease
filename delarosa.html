PERFECT AL DENTE! The sauce is deliciously creamy with a subtle tang that keeps it from feeling too heavy. It coats every piece of pasta beautifully, with just the right amount of cheese. No overpowering bitter vodka taste, which is a plus! The only downside? The bacon is a little too overpowering and competes with the sauce.

ATMOSPHERE

Quiet and uncrowded, but still has that cozy, welcoming feel. The perfect spot for a solo date, especially on a rainy day when all you want is good pasta and a little comfort.

SERVICE

Excellent service! The staff was incredibly friendly, attentive, and quick. They regularly checked in to make sure everything was going well without being overbearing.

LAST THOUGHTS

A great bowl of rigatoni that gets most things right. Perfectly al dente pasta, a deliciously creamy sauce, and service that doesn't miss. The bacon was a little too dominant, but the cozy atmosphere and quick seating made up for it. Would I come back? Absolutely.
